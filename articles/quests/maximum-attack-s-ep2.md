# Maximum Attack S [Ep.2]

Maximum Attack S [Ep.2] is a quest that takes place in the nightime version of Seaside Area, and its daytime counterpart. It consists of various waves of enemy "spam", including CCA's minibosses in the second part of the quest, making it one of the hardest non-Destiny quest on the server.

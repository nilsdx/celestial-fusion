# Training Room

Training Room is a quest that can be used to benchmark damages your character deal as well as test out your loadout.

## Recoboxes

This quest is comprised of many recoboxes in all 3 Ruins floors. They are all immune to both Demon's and Hell. They all have 3,000,000 HP, and modified defensive stats depending on their floor:
- Ruins 1: 2,800 DFP | 1,000 EVP | 50 to all resistances
- Ruins 2: 1,200 DFP | 800 EVP | 30 to all resistances
- Ruins 3: 0 DFP | 0 EVP | 0 to all resistances

## Delbiter

There is a room that contains a buffed Delbiter with 3,000,000 HP.

## Meseta NPC

By warping back to Pioneer 2, you can talk to the FOnewm next to the bank and he will max out your Mesetas to 999,999, thus removing the necessity to farm Mesetas entirely. You can talk to him as many times as you want to fill out your banks and your character's meseta balance.

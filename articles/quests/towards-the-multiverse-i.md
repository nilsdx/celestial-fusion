# Toward the Multiverse [Lv.I]

Toward the Multiverse [Lv.I] (often abreviated as TTM1) is a quest that's part of the Toward the Multiverse serie. Similar to Towards the Future and Respective Tomorrow, it is a boss rush quest, except you fight every single boss across episode 1 and 2 in one go.

The bosses's drops have been modified or removed, thus making this quest not a good option to farm boss drops.

This quest can only be played in Ultimate, and you can't go back in if you die and teleport back to Pioneer II. Furthermore, Exp. gained from these bosses is toned down.

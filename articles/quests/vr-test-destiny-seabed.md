# VR Test Destiny: Seabed

VR Test Destiny: Seabed is a quest that takes place in all 2 parts of [Olga Flow](/enemies/olga-flow)'s arena.

## Rewards

After clearing the quest, you can talk to Elenor next to the shop gates to randomly acquire one item from a list, with 3 different tier of rarity.
All weapons come with with two random attributes between 50 and 60%, and hit between 30 and 65%.

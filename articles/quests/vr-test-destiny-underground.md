# VR Test Destiny: Underground

## Teleporting gates

Various rooms in this quest uses CCA Gates that teleports you to the other side of the arena.

## Secret boss

After beating the quest, you can find a hidden teleporter in the last area that will put you in a fight against a buffed [Gol Dragon](/enemies/gol-dragon).

## Trade NPC

After clearing the quest, you can talk to Dr. Montague in front of the shop gates to trade [Photon Tokens](/tools/photon-token) to acquire up to 5 [Legendary Crates](/tools/legendary-crate).

Beating the secret boss allows you to make another trade with him.

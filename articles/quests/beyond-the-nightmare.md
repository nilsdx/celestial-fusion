# Beyond the Nightmare

Beyond the Nightmare is a quest where you can fight against Nightmare Dominator VI, which is a super buffed version of [Dark Falz](/enemies/dark-falz), alongside Phantasmal Constructs when the boss enters its third phase.

It is currently the only quest where one can farm [Primal Photon Sphere](/tools/primal-photon-sphere).

## Custom Enemies
:::row
Nightmare Dominator VI
Phantasmal Construct
:::

# Toward the Multiverse [Lv.II]

Toward the Multiverse [Lv.II] (often abreviated as TTM2) is a quest that's part of the Toward the Multiverse serie. Similar to Towards the Future and Respective Tomorrow, it is a boss rush quest, except you fight every single boss across episode 1 and 2 in one go.

In this sequel for [Towards the Multiverse \[Lv.I\]](/quests/towards-the-multiverse-i), bosses have been significantly buffed, making it a harder challenge, but offers much better drop rates for [Heavenly Resonance](/barriers/heavenly-resonance)

This quest can only be played in Ultimate, and you can't go back in if you die and teleport back to Pioneer II. Furthermore, Exp. gained from these bosses is toned down.
